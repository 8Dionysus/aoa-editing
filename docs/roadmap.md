# Roadmap

## Stable center

Preserve the canonical edit graph, immutable version/patch law, evidence versus
decision boundary, source lineage, and common application services.

## Next growth layers

1. Background job queue with process isolation, cancellation, and resumable
   checkpoints for long ASR/segmentation/render work.
2. Bind only owner-proven semantic vision capabilities: OpenVINO-friendly
   description/regions first, segmentation/depth only where hardware, latency,
   quality, and licenses justify them.
3. Extend the implemented reviewed voiceover timing path with optional
   music/beat evidence and explicit cross-frame-rate retiming.
4. Richer timeline editing and keyframe curves in the workbench.
5. Higher-fidelity MLT effect mapping plus Resolve/Premiere adapters.
6. Blender or another 2.5D compositor adapter without changing project canon.
7. Human-governed Tree of Editing review of the transfer-backed candidate;
   promotion remains outside this repository's authority.

## Explicit prototype debt

- The workbench offers inspectable tracks, direct pin/include operations, typed
  patch editing, and point renders; it is not yet a full drag/trim/keyframe NLE.
- The baseline analyzer implements technical probe, scenes, silence, loudness,
  image statistics, and a non-semantic layer proxy. Nine AI capabilities now
  have typed aliases and fail-closed receipts, but a declaration is not a live
  provider: VAD, diarization, semantic vision/retrieval, and editorial models
  remain unavailable until a stack/machine owner registration and product eval
  prove them.
- Caption compilation, segment-level voiceover alignment, and human timing
  review exist; interactive word-level transcript surgery and automatic
  repetition decisions need dedicated evals.
- Gain keyframes support explicit ducking envelopes; automatic sidechain or
  beat-aware mixing remains a planner/analysis layer.
- Alpha is preserved inside composition, but the two baseline H.264 delivery
  profiles flatten to an explicit background. Add a tested ProRes 4444/WebM
  alpha profile before claiming transparent delivery.
- Portable motion adapts source aspect through normalized contain geometry.
  A different output aspect still requires a typed reframe operation, and
  composition applicability still needs fixture ground truth or an explicit
  human assertion before positive application.
- Kdenlive/MLT is the live-validated editable lane. OTIO is intentionally lossy
  for several effects; Resolve/Premiere adapters are future ports.
- Jobs have durable receipts and safe retry, but not yet a multi-process queue,
  checkpoint scheduler, pause, or distributed collaboration.
- The workflow-reference gate currently trusts attributable manual range
  classification and measures only deterministic frame activity. Automatic
  presenter/human detection may assist review later, but cannot silently make
  inclusion decisions. Floating-window screen staging also needs a dedicated
  video-safe layout effect and renderer eval before it becomes an executable
  template.

Target-specific reconstruction observations must not enter this roadmap as
general law until transfer evidence supports them.
